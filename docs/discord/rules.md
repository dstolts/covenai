# Discord Server Rules
**Pin this in #rules. Lock the channel so only @Guildmaster can post.**

---

## Paste into #rules

```
THE COVEN -- CODE OF CONDUCT

This is a community for AI builders. These rules exist to keep it useful, respectful, and worth your time.


1. BE CONSTRUCTIVE
   Help others learn. Answer questions. Share what you know.
   Criticism of code is welcome. Criticism of people is not.
   If someone asks a beginner question, remember you were a beginner once.

2. NO SPAM OR SELF-PROMOTION
   No unsolicited DMs, affiliate links, or server invites.
   Share your projects in #show-and-tell or #content-corner -- that is encouraged.
   Repeated low-effort posts ("great!", "nice", single-word messages) may be removed.

3. KEEP IT PROFESSIONAL
   No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks.
   No NSFW content. No doxxing. No threats.
   Political and religious debates belong elsewhere.

4. RESPECT THE CRAFT
   Credit other people's work. Do not claim someone else's code as your own.
   When sharing AI-generated code, say so.
   Plagiarism in challenge submissions = disqualification + warning.

5. STAY ON TOPIC
   Use channels for their intended purpose.
   Off-topic chat goes in #off-topic.
   Bot commands go in #bot-commands, not general channels.

6. NO GAMING THE SYSTEM
   Attempts to manipulate XP, the leaderboard, or tier status through bots,
   alt accounts, or coordinated spam will result in XP reset and possible ban.
   Earn your rank honestly or do not earn it at all.

7. ASK BEFORE YOU DM
   Do not DM members without permission. Ask in the channel first.
   Exception: moderators may DM for moderation purposes.

8. FOLLOW DISCORD'S TERMS
   Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply at all times.
   https://discord.com/guidelines


ENFORCEMENT

  1st offense:  Warning (DM from moderator)
  2nd offense:  1-hour timeout
  3rd offense:  24-hour timeout
  Severe/repeat: Ban

Moderators (@Sentinel) have final say. Appeals go to @Guildmaster.
Spam bots and raid accounts are banned immediately without warning.

By participating in The Coven, you agree to these rules.
```
