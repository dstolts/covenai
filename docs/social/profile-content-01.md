## LinkedIn (CovenAI Company Page)

**Company Name:** CovenAI

**About Section (2000 chars):**
CovenAI is an open-source community engagement framework built for people who care about two things equally: community and AI.

A coven is a gathering of people bound by shared knowledge and purpose. CovenAI is a gathering of builders -- developers, creators, and leaders who believe AI should lift everyone, not just the few who can afford it.

The framework includes a complete gamification system with medieval-themed ranks (Apprentice through Legend), weekly league competitions, team-based challenges, and cross-platform engagement tracking. Every action earns recognition -- from a thoughtful comment to a merged pull request to a tutorial helping someone deploy their first AI project.

What makes CovenAI different:

- Community First: Every feature rewards people who help others succeed. Thread starters earn more than solo commenters. Teams earn more than individuals. Quality over quantity.

- Open Source, Open Knowledge: The entire framework is free. Any community, creator, or company can deploy their own Coven. We do not gatekeep how we build.

- AI for Everyone: AI is the most powerful tool humanity has ever built. It should not be locked behind enterprise paywalls. CovenAI rewards people who teach, demonstrate, and share practical AI knowledge.

- Earn by Contributing: Your rank reflects your impact on the community, not your tenure or title.

- Ship Over Talk: We value builders who ship real things. The highest tiers are earned by people who build AND teach, not just one or the other.

CovenAI tracks engagement across GitHub, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, and Reddit. Contributions are scored, ranked, and displayed on public leaderboards. Weekly leagues promote and demote members based on activity, keeping competition fresh.

The tier system runs from Apprentice (Tier 0) to Legend (Tier 7). Annual rolling badges mean a 2026 Legend badge is permanent forever -- early contributors are recognized for life.

CovenAI is built and maintained by Just In Time AI (jitai.co).

Join free at covenai.co.
