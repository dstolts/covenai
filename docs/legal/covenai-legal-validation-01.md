## 7. "COVEN" AS A GENERIC/DESCRIPTIVE TERM

"Coven" is a common English dictionary word defined as:
- "A collection of individuals with similar interests or activities" (Merriam-Webster)
- "An assembly or band of usually 13 witches" (primary definition)
- Etymology: Anglo-Norman "covent" from Latin "conventum" (convention/gathering)

### Protectability Analysis

- "Coven" is a SUGGESTIVE mark when used for community/gathering services -- it suggests the nature of the service (a gathering) but requires imagination to connect. Suggestive marks are protectable but weaker.
- The Coven Minneapolis likely has rights to "The Coven" as a suggestive mark in the coworking/community space, but NOT exclusive rights to the word "coven" across all industries.
- Adding "AI" as a suffix creates a new composite mark ("CovenAI") that is more distinctive than "Coven" alone.
- Multiple entities already use "Coven" in their branding (Coven Works, Coven Labs, Code Coven, Coven Beauty, Coven Brewing, etc.), demonstrating that no single entity owns exclusive rights to the word across industries.

**Verdict: FAVORABLE** -- "Coven" is too common and widely used for any single entity to block "CovenAI" in the software/AI space. The compound mark is sufficiently distinctive.

### Rating: CAUTION (Proceed with Mitigation)

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |

### Recommended Immediate Actions

1. **Register github.com/covenai as an organization** -- available NOW, could be taken at any time
2. **Manually check and register @covenai** on Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube
3. **Consider acquiring covenai.com** -- currently for sale on GoDaddy, expires/expired 2026-03-20
