# CovenAI -- Brand Identity
**Created:** 2026-03-24
**Status:** DRAFT v1

## What Is CovenAI?

CovenAI is an open-source community engagement framework built for people who care about two things equally: **community** and **AI**.

A coven is a gathering of people bound by shared knowledge and purpose. CovenAI is a gathering of builders -- developers, creators, and leaders who believe AI should lift everyone, not just the few who can afford it.

## Mission

**Grow together. Build with AI. Give back.**

CovenAI exists to connect AI builders into a community where sharing knowledge is rewarded, helping others is valued, and the best ideas come from collaboration, not isolation.

## Values

### 1. Community First
We are builders who lift other builders. Every feature of CovenAI is designed to reward people who help others succeed -- not just people who accumulate points for themselves. Thread starters earn more than solo commenters. Teams earn more than individuals. The referral system rewards quality (did your referral stick and contribute?) not quantity.

### 2. Open Source, Open Knowledge
The CovenAI framework is free and open source. Any community, creator, or company can deploy their own Coven. Knowledge shared freely comes back multiplied. We don't gatekeep how we build -- the "how" of CovenAI itself is open for anyone to use, adapt, and improve.

### 3. AI for Everyone
AI is the most powerful tool humanity has ever built. It should not be locked behind enterprise paywalls or understood only by researchers. CovenAI makes AI accessible by rewarding people who teach, demonstrate, and share practical AI knowledge with their communities.

### 4. Earn by Contributing
Every action in the Coven earns recognition -- from a thoughtful comment to a merged pull request to a blog post helping someone deploy their first AI project. Your rank reflects your impact on the community, not your tenure or title.

### 5. Ship Over Talk
We value builders who ship real things over theorists who debate. Challenges reward completed projects. Teams form around working code. The highest tier in the Coven is earned by people who build AND teach, not just one or the other.

## Voice and Tone

**Expert but never elitist.** We know our stuff, but we explain it so anyone can follow.
**Fast-paced but never rushed.** Every word earns its place. No filler, no fluff.
**Mystical but grounded.** The medieval aesthetic is fun and memorable, but the content is practical and real.
**Bold but not arrogant.** We make strong claims and back them up with demos.
**Inclusive but standards-driven.** Everyone is welcome. Low floor, high ceiling. Beginners start as Apprentices and grow through real contribution.

### Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic:** Medieval/mystical meets modern terminal
- **Color palette:** Deep purple (#5B21B6), gold (#F59E0B), dark slate (#1E293B), emerald (#10B981)
- **Icon concept:** Mystical symbol (eye, flame, or geometric sigil) with code brackets `</>` integrated
- **Font direction:** Modern monospace for code elements, serif or blackletter accent for headings
- **Never:** Cartoon witches, broomsticks, Halloween imagery, pentagrams (stay classy, stay tech)

- Medieval/folk sonic logo (3-5 seconds)

## Target Audiences

### Primary: AI Builders (developers who use AI coding tools)
- Use Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, or similar daily
- Want to level up their AI workflow
- Value practical demos over theory
- Active on YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Discord

### Secondary: AI-Curious Professionals (non-developers exploring AI)
- Business leaders, ops people, content creators
- Want to understand what AI can do for their work
- 63% of "vibe coding" users are non-developers -- this audience is huge
- Active on LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok

### Tertiary: Community Builders (creators who want to gamify their own communities)
- Deploy CovenAI as an open-source framework for their own audience
- Dev rel teams, community managers, content creators
- Every deployment = another distribution channel for the CovenAI brand

## Positioning

### What CovenAI Is
- A community for AI builders who give back
- An open-source framework anyone can deploy
- A gamification system that rewards real contribution
- A gathering place for people who build with AI and teach others

### What CovenAI Is NOT
- Not a course or certification program (though members can earn credentials)
- Not a company or product to buy (it's open source and free)
- Not exclusive to Claude Code users (any AI builder is welcome)
- Not a cult or secret society (the medieval theme is fun branding, not ideology)
